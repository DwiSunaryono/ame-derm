# Results

The unprocessed per-fold and per-sample outputs underlying several of the
paper's numbers, along with a script that converts them into CSV tables.
There is no training, no GPU, and no pipeline re-run — only arithmetic on data
already present.

## Contents

| Path | Description |
|---|---|
| [`data/panderm_baseline/generalist_fold*_seed42_test.csv`](data/panderm_baseline) | **The raw data behind the headline result.** PanDerm's own per-sample test predictions (`true_label`/`predicted_label`), 5 folds at seed 42 — taken without modification from the vendored PanDerm repo's `results/rigorous_5fold/`. |
| [`data/panderm_baseline/generalist_fold*_seed122_test.csv`](data/panderm_baseline) | The same at a second seed (122), backing the paper's seed-robustness claim (§"Comparison with a Dermatology Foundation Model"). |

| [`data/moe_champion_5fold_results.json`](data/moe_champion_5fold_results.json) | The raw output of `ame_derm.moe_fusion.eval_moe_champion_5fold`: per-fold macro-F1 for the single generalist and for the symmetric (base-expert) static MoE. |
| [`data/split_audit.json`](data/split_audit.json) | The raw output of `ame_derm.data_splits.audit_split_representativeness`: per-fold, per-field (label/dataset/sex/localization/age) distribution-gap and coverage statistics for the lesion-grouped 5-fold split. |

## Reproduce

```bash
cd results
python scripts/reproduce_tables.py
```

## What's raw and what isn't

**`panderm_comparison.csv` is raw on both sides.** PanDerm's macro-F1 is
computed here from its own per-sample predictions and comes out at 75.34 —
matching the manuscript to four decimal places. It is likewise the paper's
strongest result: 5 of 5 folds positive, exact sign test p=0.031. Consider
this the one most deserving of trust.

**`moe_champion_5fold_results.json` is raw as well.** Its `gen_only_f1` and
`champ_static_f1` columns are exactly what
`ame_derm.moe_fusion.eval_moe_champion_5fold` produced; `reproduce_tables.py`
merely computes the mean and std, with nothing hand-typed.

**The AME-Derm row in `main_ablation_ladder.csv` is the lone exception.** The
asymmetric model's own per-fold macro-F1 was never recovered from the archived
logs, so that row equals `champ_static_f1` plus the per-fold delta reported by
the manuscript ({+1.33, +0.71, +0.73, +1.81, −0.37} points) — a reconstruction
rather than a fresh measurement (see `../docs/SYSTEM_DOCUMENTATION.md` for how
this came about). The significance test is unaffected, however:
[`../evaluation/statistical_significance.py`](../evaluation/statistical_significance.py)
needs only that delta array, and running it reproduces the manuscript's
t(4)=2.30, p=0.041 (paired t-test) and p≈0.062 (Wilcoxon) exactly.

**`external_validation_bcn.csv` is raw too** — a fresh
`sklearn.metrics.f1_score(labs, preds, average="macro")` computed over each
run's actual per-sample predictions, not copied from the paper in any form.
